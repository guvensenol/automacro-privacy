AutoMacro Privacy Policy

App: AutoMacro: Image Auto Clicker (com.guvensenol.automacro)
Effective date: October 2, 2026

Short version: AutoMacro does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send data off your device.

1. Data we collect

None. AutoMacro has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no cloud services. The app does not request the Android internet permission; you can verify this in your phone's Settings → Apps → AutoMacro → Permissions.

2. Accessibility Service

AutoMacro uses the Android Accessibility Service API only to perform the taps, swipes, gestures and text input that you define in your own macros, and to find on-screen elements (for example a list row or a piece of text) that your macro step refers to. Screen content read this way is used only at that moment, in memory, to run the step. It is not saved, logged or transmitted.

3. Screen capture

When a macro uses image matching or on-screen text recognition, the app captures the screen with your permission (Android's screen capture prompt). Captured frames are processed on the device and discarded. Only template images that you explicitly capture while editing a macro are saved, and they stay in the app's storage on your phone.

4. Screen recording and microphone

AutoMacro can record a video of your screen when you start a recording yourself. If you turn on the optional "Record audio" setting (off by default), the recording also includes sound from the microphone. Recordings are saved only on your device and are never uploaded or shared by the app.

5. Data stored on your device

Your macros, template images, screen recordings, saved texts, settings and any backup files you create are stored only on your device. Backups are created only when you choose to make one, and you decide where to save them. Uninstalling the app removes its stored data.

6. Purchases

AutoMacro Pro is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details. The app only receives the purchase status from the Google Play Store app on your device and verifies it on the device. Google's handling of purchase data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer any user data to third parties, because we do not collect any.

8. Children

AutoMacro is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
